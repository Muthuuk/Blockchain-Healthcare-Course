Quick guide

How the course works

Each chapter takes about 30 to 45 minutes and can be done in short bursts. Your place is saved automatically on this device.

1. Start

Answer three quick questions to see what you already know, choose a narrator voice and speed, and turn the voice off if you prefer to read the captions.

2. Watch the 3D lesson

Press play. Maya narrates while the 3D scene builds step by step, for example a chain of blocks that turns red when one record is tampered with. Drag the scene to look around. Use ⏮ ⏭ to move one sentence at a time, CC for captions, ⛶ for full screen. The full transcript sits under the video.

The voice comes from your device. If it sounds robotic, try another voice on the Start page. Microsoft Edge and Safari usually offer natural British voices.

3. Quick check and scenario

Say whether you are sure, then answer one question. Wrong first time? You get a second, easier question. Then decide a two-step healthcare scenario. The best choice earns 2 points, a partly right one earns 1.

4. Hands-on labs

Some lessons end with a lab: hash a record with real SHA-256, mine blocks, sign a prescription with a real key pair, race a 51% attacker, step through a re-entrancy attack and more. Labs never send data anywhere.

5. Final test, learning index and certificate

Each chapter ends with a scenario-based final test. Your learning index (0 to 100) combines:

  • Knowledge (35%): first-try quick checks and final-test answers.
  • Applying it (35%): points earned in scenarios.
  • Self-awareness (15%): how often your confidence matched your answer.
  • Completion (15%): lessons finished and the final test taken.

Levels: Novice (0–39), Advanced beginner (40–59), Competent (60–79), Proficient (80+). Finish all lessons and the final test to print a certificate.

Privacy

Everything is stored in your browser's local storage. Clearing your browser data, or using another device, starts afresh.
