Course by Prof Muthu Ramachandran, based on the Springer book Blockchain Engineering in Healthcare. Your progress is stored in this browser only; nothing is sent anywhere. Use the same browser and device to keep your record, and download a certificate from each chapter. Narration uses your device's built-in voices. 3D graphics use three.js (MIT licence).
